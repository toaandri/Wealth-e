5. FINANCIAL ACCOUNTING RULES (CRITICAL)
Define a documented transaction sign convention and immutable posted transaction ledger; corrections use reversal/adjustment, not silent edits. Separate cash flow, market value, income, fees, transfers, FX effects, liabilities and price appreciation. A transfer between accounts owned by same portfolio has zero portfolio-level external cash flow and must not double count; paired transfer IDs and reconciliation. Buying securities decreases cash and increases units; selling decreases units and increases cash net of fees; dividends increase cash and investment income; split changes quantity without creating wealth; fees reduce net wealth; loan proceeds increase cash AND liability equally; principal repayment decreases cash and liability equally, interest expense decreases wealth. For holdings: market value = quantity * quote price in quote currency, converted using FX as-of timestamp; historical prices/FX matched using last-known-at-or-before with configurable staleness, never future observations. Gross assets = cash + investments + property + other assets, avoiding duplicate asset accounts/holdings; net worth = gross assets - outstanding liabilities. Missing or stale prices must be flagged and never silently treated as fresh or zero. Property valuations are estimates, not market trades. Define manual overrides with provenance. FX conversion amount_target = amount_source * rate(source,target); inverse only when mathematically valid, triangulation with documented rules; round at display boundary where possible, use consistent precision and currency minor units (MGA may require provider-specific conventions). Never sum mixed currencies without converting to base currency. Separate portfolio change from investment performance: net worth delta = market/FX/valuation effects + external flows + income - expenses and other categorized effects, with reconciliation residual exposed. Time-weighted return requires external flow segmentation; money-weighted XIRR requires robust convergence/failure handling; do not claim performance if missing histories. Unrealized P/L = market value - remaining cost basis; realized P/L uses explicitly selected FIFO or average-cost policy, document method, fees and FX treatment. CAGR only for valid positive starting values and time intervals. Annualized projections are illustrative scenarios, not predictions: FV lump sum P(1+r)^n and contributions with explicit deposit timing, compounding, inflation and fees; handle r=0 and negative rates; provide scenario ranges, never guaranteed outcomes. Test zero, negative, extreme, missing, duplicates, stale FX, split, multi-currency, transfers, deposits, loan amortization and rounding. Backend is source of truth; web/mobile only format values.
